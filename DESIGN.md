# BESTARI design

This records the visual choices already present in the site and the accessibility changes authorized after the October 2026 UI audit. It is maintenance guidance inferred from this implementation, not a historical brand brief.

BESTARI is an independent social foundation rooted in Muai Village and the Belayan River region. The site introduces its work to communities and prospective institutional partners. Local field photographs and aerial footage connect the text to that place; they are the main visual identity, rather than generic illustrations or decorative effects.

ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

- Energy stays calm so community work and partnership information remain the focus.
- Rhythm varies between the homepage's split hero, the story's image and text, the work index, and partnership contact details. Related sections share spacing and typography.
- Motion is limited to interaction feedback and the homepage's contextual aerial footage. Video can be paused; reduced motion uses the still image without loading video. No decorative loops or scroll choreography.

Navy grounds the header, footer, and primary actions. Warm gold marks the homepage's primary partnership action and small supporting accents. Paper and mist distinguish reading sections without adding competing colors. Navy overlays and caption scrims keep field imagery behind readable text. Gold is not a hover text color on light backgrounds.

Source Serif 4 gives headings and the foundation name a consistent editorial voice. Roboto keeps body text and navigation compact and readable. Use the existing type scale rather than adding another font.

The four strategic pillars receive equal treatment because the content presents them as equally important areas of work. Their regular grid is an index, not a ranking. Other sections use compositions appropriate to their content.

Keep surfaces mostly flat, buttons nearly square, and generous curves on selected images. The curved hero image is a repeated identity gesture; it should not spread to every component. The mobile menu's shadow separates its overlay from page content.

Labels identify actions without decorative arrows. Downward chevrons signal section jumps and disclosures. The outward arrow is reserved for the WhatsApp destination; the telephone icon identifies calls.

Interactive targets have at least 44px of height and width. Keyboard focus remains visible. Preserve the existing responsive stacking, bilingual destinations, honest portfolio notice, and disclosure of developing governance systems.
